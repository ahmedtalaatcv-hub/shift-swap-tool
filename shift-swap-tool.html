Shift Swap Validator

Validation rules

⏱️
Rest periodAt least 12 hours between shift end and next shift start. PM end → PM start counts as the next day.
📆
Consecutive daysAt most 6 working days in a row across both weeks.
🗓️
Working weekStarts Sunday, ends Saturday.
🕘
Shift durationNormal shifts are 9 hours.
🕌
Ramadan7-hour shifts, set per employee.
⚠️
ExemptionsOff on Saturday (day 7) in the current week, or Sunday (day 8) in the next week, waives the 12-hour rule.
🔢
Day numbersCurrent week Sun=1 … Sat=7. Next week Sun=8 … Sat=14.
